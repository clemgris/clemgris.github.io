I am Clémence Grislain, a third-year Ph.D. student at [ISIR](https://www.isir.upmc.fr/) (Institut des Systèmes Intelligents et de Robotique), [Sorbonne Université](https://www.sorbonne-universite.fr/), supervised by [Olivier Sigaud](https://www.isir.upmc.fr/personnel/sigaud/) and [Mohamed Chetouani](https://www.isir.upmc.fr/personnel/chetouani/).

🤖 My research focuses on robot learning: reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and language-conditioned control, with a particular interest in diffusion policies, vision-language(-action) models, and robotic failure detection.

Before my Ph.D., I was a visiting research intern at the [Whiteson Research Lab (WhiRL)](https://whirl.cs.ox.ac.uk/), University of Oxford, working with [Shimon Whiteson](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/shimon.whiteson/) on the imitation gap in autonomous driving, and a research intern at ISIR working on Bayesian Theory of Mind for adaptive teaching.

I graduated from [École des Ponts ParisTech](https://ecoledesponts.fr/) in 2023 and hold a master's degree from [ENS Paris-Saclay](https://ens-paris-saclay.fr/) in Mathematics, Vision, and Machine Learning ([MVA](https://www.master-mva.com/)).

Feel free to reach out for possible collaborations or questions regarding my research!
